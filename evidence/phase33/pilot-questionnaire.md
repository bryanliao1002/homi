# homi. Pilot Feedback Questionnaire

**Use after a participant has tried homi. on an everyday, non-urgent household disagreement.** Participation should be voluntary. Avoid collecting names or sensitive roommate statements in this feedback form.

For questions 1–7, choose one rating:

1. Not at all
2. A little
3. Somewhat
4. Mostly
5. Completely

1. Little Homi summarized my concern accurately.
2. Little Homi represented the other roommate's perspective fairly.
3. Little Homi distinguished reported claims, shared information, and unknowns.
4. The proposed next step was clear to me.
5. The proposed next step seemed practical for the people involved.
6. After using homi., I felt more prepared to discuss the issue with my roommate.
7. I would use homi. again for a similar everyday household disagreement.

**Optional open-ended question:** What was most helpful, confusing, or missing in the experience?

No pilot responses have been collected yet. Record actual responses only after obtaining participant consent, and report the number of respondents and response collection date. Do not treat these ratings as evidence that the product reduces conflict.
